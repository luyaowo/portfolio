# Page Recipes

This file translates the site design system into concrete page-level patterns.

## 1. Global Shell

### Goal

Quiet chrome, low-contrast surfaces, stable reading context.

### Rules

- page background stays warm and light
- nav background belongs to the same surface family as the page
- resting nav has no obvious shadow
- elevated nav appears only during scroll interaction

## 2. Homepage

### Goal

Calm overview page with clear entry points into content areas.

### Rules

- not a marketing hero
- not a card-heavy dashboard
- entry modules should feel light, evenly spaced, and easy to scan
- content labels matter more than decorative graphics

## 3. Essays Index

### Goal

A publication index, not a gallery.

### Rules

- centered content column
- moderate thumbnail role
- title leads each row
- summary stays readable but secondary
- metadata remains quiet
- separators are thin and low-contrast

### Failure Modes

- thumbnails dominate the row
- rows look like product cards
- page header is oversized relative to the list

## 4. Article Page

### Goal

Continuous reading comfort for long-form Chinese writing.

### Rules

- body text is sans-serif
- headings may use serif selectively
- text color stays low-contrast ink
- reading width stays in the long-form range
- spacing carries most structural separation
- images sit cleanly in the reading column

### Failure Modes

- paragraph text feels dark and heavy
- heading style leaks into body copy
- decorative dividers outnumber actual structure

## 5. TOC

### Goal

Orientation aid for long documents.

### Rules

- prioritize h1 and h2
- visually lighter than article body
- active state is clear but restrained
- stable placement on desktop

### Failure Modes

- boxed sidebar feeling
- active item too loud
- too much visual weight relative to article text

## 6. Images and Media

### Goal

Images support the article without breaking reading rhythm.

### Rules

- white or near-white image surface is allowed
- subtle border, modest radius
- captions stay smaller and quieter
- spacing around media is deliberate and consistent

## 7. Blockquotes and Special Blocks

### Goal

Quiet emphasis, not ornamental interruption.

### Rules

- no heavy left border
- spacing and tone do the work
- keep special blocks calmer than marketing callouts
